Excerpts and simplifications, retrieved from GOV.UK on 2026-10-27.

[AA-1] You must have reached State Pension age. (source: https://www.gov.uk/attendance-allowance/eligibility)

[AA-2] You must have a physical disability (including sensory disability), a mental disability (including learning difficulties) or a health condition that is severe enough that you need help caring for yourself or someone to supervise you, for your own or someone else's safety. (source: https://www.gov.uk/attendance-allowance/eligibility)

[AA-3] You must "have needed that help for at least 6 months". (source: https://www.gov.uk/attendance-allowance/eligibility)

[AA-4] You must "have been in Great Britain for at least 2 of the last 3 years". (source: https://www.gov.uk/attendance-allowance/eligibility)

[AA-5] You cannot get Attendance Allowance if you already get Disability Living Allowance (DLA), Personal Independence Payment (PIP) or Adult Disability Payment. (source: https://www.gov.uk/attendance-allowance/eligibility)

[AA-6] Attendance Allowance is paid at £76.70 a week (lower rate) or £114.60 a week (higher rate), depending on the level of care you need. Simplification: higher rate when help is needed both day and night, otherwise lower rate. (source: https://www.gov.uk/attendance-allowance)

[AA-7] People living in Scotland apply for Pension Age Disability Payment instead. This scheme covers England and Wales. (source: https://www.gov.uk/attendance-allowance)
