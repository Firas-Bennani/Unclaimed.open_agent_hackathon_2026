Excerpts and simplifications, retrieved from GOV.UK on 2026-10-27.

[PC-1] "You must live in England, Scotland or Wales and have reached State Pension age to qualify for Pension Credit." (source: https://www.gov.uk/pension-credit/eligibility)

[PC-2] "You must include your partner on your application. You're eligible if you and your partner have both reached State Pension age, or if one of you gets Housing Benefit for people over State Pension age." Simplification: for a couple we require both partners to have reached State Pension age. (source: https://www.gov.uk/pension-credit/eligibility)

[PC-3] "If you have £10,000 or less in savings and investments this will not affect your Pension Credit." For savings above £10,000, every £500 (or part of £500) over that threshold is counted as £1 of weekly income. (source: https://www.gov.uk/pension-credit/eligibility)

[PC-4] Guarantee Credit tops up "your weekly income to £238 if you're single" or "your joint weekly income to £363.25 if you have a partner". A household is eligible when its assessed weekly income (income plus the income counted from savings) is below that amount. (source: https://www.gov.uk/pension-credit/what-youll-get)

[PC-5] The weekly amount of Guarantee Credit is the difference between £238 (single) or £363.25 (couple) and the assessed weekly income. (source: https://www.gov.uk/pension-credit/what-youll-get)

Simplification (State Pension age): people born before 6 April 1960 reach State Pension age at 66; people born on or after 6 April 1960 are treated as reaching it at 67 (the GOV.UK transitional months are ignored).
