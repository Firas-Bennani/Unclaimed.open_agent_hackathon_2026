Excerpts and simplifications, retrieved from GOV.UK on 2026-10-27.

[WHD-1] This scheme covers England and Wales. Scotland has separate rules and Northern Ireland is not covered. (source: https://www.gov.uk/the-warm-home-discount-scheme)

[WHD-2] "You may be eligible if all of the following applied on 23 August 2026: ... you (or your partner) get certain means-tested benefits". The benefits are Pension Credit, Housing Benefit, Universal Credit and income-related Employment and Support Allowance. (source: https://www.gov.uk/the-warm-home-discount-scheme/if-you-live-in-england-and-wales)

[WHD-3] "your name (or your partner's) is on the electricity bill" on 23 August 2026. (source: https://www.gov.uk/the-warm-home-discount-scheme/if-you-live-in-england-and-wales)

[WHD-4] The scheme is "a one-off £150 discount off your electricity bill". (source: https://www.gov.uk/the-warm-home-discount-scheme)

Simplification: we assume the household's energy supplier is part of the scheme.
